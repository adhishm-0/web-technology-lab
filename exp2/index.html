About Astrology

Why the stars matter for your destiny

A modern guide to astrology that blends tradition, history, and performance wisdom for real life.

What is Astrology?

Astrology is the study of planetary and star alignments that influence personality, timing, and emotional patterns.

History

From ancient Mesopotamia to modern digital horoscopes, astrology has evolved across cultures and centuries.

Western Astrology

Western astrology is based on sun signs, houses, and planetary transits to paint your personality map.

Vedic Astrology

Vedic astrology uses lunar constellations and nakshatras to reveal destiny, karmic cycles, and spiritual lessons.

Chinese Astrology

Chinese astrology combines zodiac animals, elements, and lunar years to create a cultural life forecast.

Benefits

  1. Self-awareness
  2. Better timing
  3. Relationship insight
  4. Career guidance

Birth Chart

Your celestial fingerprint

Birth charts map the planets at your exact time and place of birth. They are the key to personal astrology.

What it reveals

Sun sign, moon sign, rising sign, planetary houses, and natal aspects combine to show your unique life path.

Why it matters

Understanding your chart gives clarity to strengths, challenges, and karmic lessons.

Core components

  • Sun - identity
  • Moon - emotions
  • Rising - first impression
  • Houses - life areas
