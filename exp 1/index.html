About Astrology

Astrology is the study of how celestial bodies influence human life. Your zodiac sign is determined by your birth date and reveals your personality traits and future predictions.

What is a Horoscope?

A horoscope is a personalized prediction based on your zodiac sign. It can tell you about your day, week, or year ahead and guide you in making decisions.

Four Elements

  • Fire: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius - Passionate and energetic
  • Earth: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn - Practical and grounded
  • Air: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius - Intellectual and social
  • Water: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces - Emotional and intuitive
